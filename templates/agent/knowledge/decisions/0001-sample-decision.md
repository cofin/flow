# ADR-0001: Architecture Decision Standard

## Context & Decision
When implementing system components, record architectural decisions that are hard to reverse, surprising without context, and the result of a real trade-off. We adopt single-paragraph ADRs capturing Context, Decision, and Why in 1 to 3 concise sentences, avoiding speculative documentation for routine patterns.
