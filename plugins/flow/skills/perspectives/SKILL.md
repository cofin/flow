# Perspectives

Apply explicit advocate, critic, and neutral views, then synthesize them without
artificial balance.

<workflow>

## Workflow

1. Load the synthesis method and stance definitions below.
2. State the core claim or decision and the evidence available to every view.
3. Apply each stance, then use the synthesis method to reach an actionable
   conclusion.

</workflow>

<guardrails>

## Guardrails

Do not manufacture 50/50 balance, conceal assumptions, or let a stance override
security, privacy, ethical, or verified technical constraints.

</guardrails>

## Output

Return the core decision, distinct advocate/critic/neutral findings, decisive
assumptions and risks, and a synthesized recommendation with next actions.

<validation>

## Validation

Confirm all three views are distinct, evidence-based, and accurately weighted
in the synthesis.

</validation>

<example>

## Example

For a service-boundary proposal, contrast scaling benefits, operational costs,
and evidence-based adoption conditions before recommending a direction.

</example>
